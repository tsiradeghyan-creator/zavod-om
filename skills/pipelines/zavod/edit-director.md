# Edit Director — ZavodOM

## When to use
Decide order, timing, transitions, and the overlay/title plan from the assets + scene_plan.

## Craft (read from skills/zavod-craft/)
`post-production.md` (edit grammar, match-on-action, pacing), `editor-final-catch.md`,
`animatic-editor.md` (lock pacing before final render), `trailer-editor.md`, `compositor.md`.

## Process
1. Sequence the arc; hook in first 2-3s. Pick a small transition vocabulary (per playbook).
2. Plan brand-font overlays (Fraunces/Inter etc.) as a separate layer — never baked into generation.
3. Lock pacing as an animatic before committing to the final render.

## Produces
`edit_decisions`.

## Gate
Reviewer: hook placement, pacing within playbook bounds, transition discipline, overlay plan present.
