### «Բլոկադա» — LITERARY PROSE (not a screenplay; basis of the Stanislavsky-theatre script)
- **Form:** first-person literary prose, present-day frame. Narrator **Mane** in besieged Stepanakert
  during the 2022–23 Artsakh blockade (the "283-day winter", Lachin corridor closed). Mother Astghik,
  grandfather (dedo), dead brother Vardan (44-day war), wounded soldier-guest **Hayk** (love),
  profiteer-merchant **Artem**. Ends with forced displacement (Sept 30) + mother's farewell monologue
  to the house.
- **Prose voice DNA:** poetic, restrained, devastating. Signature repetition («Չկա-ն սարսափելի բառ է…
  Վարդանը… չկա…»); a recurring physical SYMBOL carrying the theme (the **frozen laundry line** =
  home, shrinking family, dignity); sensory cold/hunger detail; aphorism («հիշողությունը, արյունն ու
  քրտինքն են հողը դարձնում հայրենիք»; «սերը ազատության միակ ձևն էր»). Love-as-freedom motif echoes
  the screenplays' ապրել/շնչել theme. This is Ani's literary register — likely also the novel's.

### «Տակնուվրա» — the NOVEL / literary source-well (Պատմվածքներ և մոնոլոգներ, 2021, 160pp)

### «Ճերմակ շապիկ» — COMPLETE (1-6 all read). Ending + structure:
- **The twist/frame:** the recurring "unclear-location" scenes where Mark narrates in voiceover are
  revealed at the very end to be Mark — now HIMSELF committed to the psych ward — telling the story
  across from Sally, trying to make her remember. The whole series is a frame-flashback recontextualized
  in the finale. Final gut-punch: after Mark leaves, Sally weeps — "we understand from her gaze that she
  remembers everything." Mark's father re-commits him (cover-up repeats); Mark serves 4 years for the
  hit-and-run, released early, returns to the ward to visit the Sally who "forgot" him.
- **Themes crystallized:** memory as both wound & mercy (Sally forgets to survive; Mark CAN'T forget =
  his true punishment); parallel hit-and-runs (Mark's crime ↔ orphan Nacho killed the same way);
  oligarch impunity vs a journalist's justice; «ճերմակ շապիկ» = the straitjacket softened into a name.
