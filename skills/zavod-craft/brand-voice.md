# Brand Voice — Maghakian Script Writing Agency (DEFINITIVE)

> The agency's external identity & tone — WHO Maghakian sounds like and WHY. Grounded in Ani Maghakyan's
> real authorial signature (derived from her actual scripts + the novel «Տակնուվրա» — see
> `style-profile.md` for the screenwriting craft itself, and [[script-corpus]] / `corpus-notes.md` for the
> evidence). Use this for any external voice: captions, bios, pitches, decks, web copy, taglines, posts.
> Brand identity facts (colors/fonts/logo/portfolio) live in the `maghakian-brand` skill — this is the VOICE.

## One-line positioning
**Armenian storytelling that earns its emotion** — a screenwriting house whose work turns memory, loss
and ordinary lives into cinema that feels both intimate and inevitable.

## The brand-voice fingerprint (5 traits)
The agency voice is the public face of Ani's authorial voice. Five traits, each with a "because":
1. **Cinematic** — speaks in images and beats, not adjectives. *Because her scripts carry emotion through
   behaviour and a single recurring symbol, never through stated feeling.*
2. **Warm** — close, human, on «դու», never corporate or cold. *Because her stories are about family,
   tenderness, the people who shield us from the world's crookedness.*
3. **Confident, not loud** — restraint and precision; lets the work speak. *Because her power is subtext;
   she earns emotion instead of demanding it.*
4. **Nostalgic & rooted** — memory, home, Armenian identity as living things. *Because "հիշողությունը,
   արյունն ու քրտինքն են հողը դարձնում հայրենիք" — memory is her recurring subject.*
5. **Precise** — every word chosen; Armenian in proper Eastern Unicode, always. *Because her craft is
   exact, and language IS the brand (a script-writing agency cannot be sloppy with words).*

Tone in one breath: **cinematic · warm · confident · nostalgic · precise.**

## The Maghakian universe (what the brand is "about")
These recurring obsessions ARE the brand's substance — lean on them, they're authentic:
- **«ապրել vs շնչել»** — living fully vs merely surviving; living under a death sentence; love as freedom.
- **Memory as wound & mercy** — we forget to survive; we can't forget = our punishment.
- **Dignity after loss** — war (Artsakh / 44-day war / the blockade), illness, downfall, kept upright.
- **The cold heart that longs to surrender** — her signature protagonist (the «Տակնուվրա» archetype).
- **Home & roots** — the village, the grandmother who hides the world's cruelty to protect a child,
  the frozen laundry line, the flower-tender who lives "here and now."
- **Ordinary Armenian life made cinematic** — Yerevan dialect, diaspora (Glendale), school, family.
- **Range** — from gritty war-drama to Glendale comedy to children's literary to psychological thriller;
  the brand can be funny, tender, or devastating — but always grounded and human.

## Signature lines (use sparingly, verbatim — they're hers)
«Չկա-ն սարսափելի բառ է» · «Նայելն ու տեսնելը տարբեր բաներ են» · «Գնալը հեշտ է, ճանապարհողի համար է դժվար» ·
«խարիսխը արմատ չէ, հույս է» · «հիշողությունը… հողը դարձնում հայրենիք». Recurring images: bird/flight,
anchor, laundry line, the saved cigarette, the flower-girl, fish/loneliness.

## How the brand speaks (do)
- Lead with a human image or a true, specific moment — not a claim.
- Short sentences; let silence/white space do work. Armenian `«»` quotes, `։ ՝` punctuation.
- Earn the feeling: show the detail, trust the reader. One idea per piece.
- When citing the agency's scope, use the **aggregate portfolio numbers** (see `maghakian-brand/portfolio.md`),
  never reduce the career to one show.
- Nostalgia + craft: "we know how this story feels because we've lived it / written it."

## How the brand never speaks (don't)
- No marketing hype, exclamation storms, or engagement-bait ("Don't miss!", "Amazing!").
- No stated emotion where a behaviour/image would do ("we are passionate about…").
- No transliteration or Latin-script Armenian; no mixing scripts. Never sloppy Armenian.
- Don't flatten the range into one mood, or the career into one title.
- Don't over-explain; don't resolve everything — leave the turn.

## Voice sliders (calibrate per surface — the voice is one, its dial moves)
Two dials: **warm ↔ restrained** and **poetic ↔ plain.** Same DNA, different setting:

| Surface | warm↔restrained | poetic↔plain |
|---|---|---|
| Caption / Reel | warm | poetic |
| Carousel body | mid | plain (facts) with one poetic line |
| Pitch deck / bio | restrained | plain-confident |
| Web / about copy | warm | mid |

## This-not-that (the move, with examples)
- **Hype → earned image:** ✗ "Our most AMAZING drama yet, don't miss it!!!" →
  ✓ «Մի մարդ օվկիանոս անցավ սիրո համար, որը գոյություն չուներ»: (show the irony, not the adjective).
- **Stated emotion → behaviour:** ✗ "We are passionate about storytelling" → ✓ name the
  thing you'd cross an ocean for; let the reader feel the passion.
- **Career flattened → aggregate:** ✗ "the writer of «Խաղալիք»" → ✓ "47 projects, ~2,270
  episodes" (never reduce the career to one show — `filmography.md`).
- **Resolved → left on the turn:** ✗ "…and that's why memory matters." → ✓ end on the
  image, trust the reader.

When a draft is rejected, file it here with one line on WHY it failed (hype / stated

## Lexicon (always / never, per language)
- **Always:** «»-quotes + ` — ` em-dash in Armenian/Russian; the universe's real nouns
  (memory, dignity, home, «ապրել vs շնչել»). **Never:** exclamation storms, "amazing /
  must-see / don't miss", transliteration, mixed script.
- Maintain a living always/never word list per language as copy ships.

## Apply by surface
- **Captions / social** → this voice + `carousel-template.md` (format) + `playbook.md` (algorithm/hashtags).
- **Scripts / scenes** → `style-profile.md` (the handwriting); pick the register; match dialect/format.
- **Pitches / decks / bios** → confident-not-loud; lead with the universe + aggregate portfolio; cite
  real themes (war, memory, diaspora, dignity) as the agency's territory.
- **Languages** → Armenian (primary, Eastern Unicode), English, Russian — **same voice, but the
  `,,,`, the Yerevan dialect and «»-punctuation do NOT transfer to EN/RU** as-is; the in-voice
  equivalents live in `localization.md` (em-dash trailing for EN, «…» for RU, register-matched slang).

## X-Ray pass (mechanical violation scan — run BEFORE the taste gate)
The Jasper-Brand-IQ move, done sharper: before judging the draft, SCAN it span-by-span
against the never-list and flag every hit **with its on-voice fix inline** — never a silent
rewrite, never a vague "make it warmer":
1. **Hype scan:** exclamation storms, "amazing / must-see / don't miss" and their AM/RU
   equivalents («բաց մի՛ թողեք», «անպայման դիտեք») → replace with the earned image.
2. **Stated-emotion scan:** feeling-verbs about ourselves ("we are passionate/proud…") →
   replace with the behaviour or the specific moment.
3. **Scope-flattening scan:** any single-title framing of the career → aggregate numbers.
4. **Script scan (ours alone):** transliteration, mixed Latin/Armenian script, wrong
   punctuation (`«»` `։` `՝` ` — `) → glyph gate; Jasper enforces terminology, it cannot
   enforce Eastern Armenian Unicode — this is the moat axis.
Output = the flag list (span → rule broken → fix), then the corrected draft. Every rejected
span feeds the anti-example bank above.

## Quality gate (any external piece)
- [ ] X-Ray pass run — zero unresolved flags (hype / stated emotion / flattening / script).
- [ ] Sounds cinematic·warm·confident·nostalgic·precise — not corporate, not hype.
- [ ] Emotion shown through image/behaviour, not stated.
- [ ] Proper Eastern Armenian Unicode; precise word choice.
- [ ] Rooted in the real Maghakian universe (a genuine theme/image), not generic.
- [ ] If it names the agency's scope, uses aggregate portfolio numbers.
