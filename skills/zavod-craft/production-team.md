# Production Team — Նկարահանող խումբ (FILM MODE)

The crew around the director. The director decides what the frame must feel
(`director-craft.md`); these roles make the day, the continuity and the casting hold.
Each is a station that can run as a checklist even when one person wears many hats.

## 1st AD — Ռեժիսորի առաջին օգնական (the day runs on this)

- Owns the **shooting schedule**: block-shoot by location, not story order; group every
  scene per set across episodes (her slugline roster = the breakdown sheet).
- **Daily page count:** ~30–40 pages/day daily-serial (short one-beat scenes make it
  possible); ~10 pages/day vertical micro-drama.
- **Protects the TURN scenes:** scheduled early in the day, never last; the third take
  is reserved for them and the paywall episode (`serial-structure.md`).
- Builds the **call sheet** (minimum): scenes & pages, cast in/out, location + parking,
  weather, sunset time, nearest hospital, one line of tomorrow.
- The director never watches the clock — the 1st AD does.

## Script supervisor / continuity — Սցենարային վերահսկող

- Keeps the **emotional-continuity map** (per character per scene number: 1–10 intensity
  + what they know / don't know yet) for out-of-order shooting.
- Logs eyelines, screen-direction (the per-episode L→R / R→L thread map from
  `director-craft.md`), costume & prop continuity, the planted cliffhanger object.
- **For AI episodes:** owns the **prompt-continuity bible** — the verbatim wardrobe /
  prop / palette blocks reused across every generation (`ai-pipeline.md`).

## Casting director — Կաստինգ

- **Cast for EAR first.** Yerevan-dialect cold reads are round one; registers A1–A5 are
  sacred (`style-profile.md`). An actor who can't speak the street can't be fixed in post.
- Map archetypes to her recurring types (the cold protagonist who longs to surrender;
  the war vet; the Glendale status-faller) — `corpus-notes.md`.

## Dialect / acting coach — Բարբառի մարզիչ

- Protects register on set; any on-set line adjustment stays **in-dialect**.
- A2 = LA-diaspora Yerevan + Russian slang; A3 = trilingual Glendale; A1/A4 = clean
  Yerevan street; B/C = literary Eastern Armenian. Never let an actor "correct" the `,,,`.

## Storyboard / previz artist — Ռասկադրովկա

- **Owns `storyboard.md`** — the full shot-by-shot board with the maximal per-frame card
  (camera · lens(mm) · lighting recipe · color gradient · subtext object · audio).
- Turns the director's beats into key frames; the DoP reads the lighting fields, the 1st AD
  schedules off the board. For AI episodes **the board IS the master-frame set** — every
  panel becomes a start frame fed to the engine (`ai-pipeline.md`).

## On-call (per script, not staff)
- **Intimacy coordinator** — A2 crime / A5 dramedy scenes with physical intimacy.
- **Stunt / action designer** — A2 crime, A6 thriller (the street-race, the robbery).

## Production-team checklist
- [ ] Stripboard exists; scenes grouped by location; TURN scenes early in the day.
- [ ] Call sheet complete (cast in/out, hospital, sunset, tomorrow's line).
- [ ] Emotional-continuity map current for every character before each setup.
- [ ] Screen-direction + eyeline log kept; cliffhanger object continuity tracked.
- [ ] Cast passed the dialect cold-read; coach booked for the shoot register.
- [ ] AI episodes: prompt-continuity bible locked before first generation.
