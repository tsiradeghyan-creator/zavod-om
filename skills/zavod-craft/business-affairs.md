# Business Affairs — Իրավունքներ և պայմանագրեր (FILM MODE)

The legal/rights/grants station. Producing (`producer-craft.md`) can't move money until
chain of title, AI rights, likeness consent and clearances are clean. None of this is
legal advice — it's the producer's checklist of what a lawyer must paper.

## Chain of title & options

- **Clean chain = signed transfer or option for every script** before any deck ships
  (her templates: «Հեղինակային իրավունքների փոխանցման պայմանագիր»).
- **Catalog-rights audit (do this first):** of the 47 projects, which does Ani control
  vs. work-for-hire owned by the channel? This answer gates the entire format-licensing
  strategy (`producer-craft.md`). Keep a one-row-per-title rights ledger.
- **Option structure:** fee 3–5% of purchase price · 12–18 month term + paid extension ·
  reversion clause · separated rights held back (publishing / stage / sequel) — esp. the
  novel «Տակնուվրա».

## AI rights (2026 reality — must be papered)

- **AI-only renders are NOT copyrightable** (US Copyright Office human-authorship rule —
  now settled at the top: SCOTUS denied cert in *Thaler v. Perlmutter*, March 2026, leaving
  human authorship as the law). **AI-ASSISTED works with documented human creative control DO
  register** — the Office has registered hundreds; the Zarya-of-the-Dawn split is the model
  (human text + arrangement protected; raw AI images not). Document the **human creative
  contribution** per project — script, prompts-as-direction, edit decisions — and **archive
  model + prompt provenance** (the seed/prompt registry in `ai-pipeline.md` is the evidence
  trail). Her 54-script authorship + the zavod's logged human gates are exactly the record
  that wins registration.
- **Verify each engine's commercial-use + indemnity terms** (Veo / Kling / Seedance /
  Nano Banana / fal-ai) before delivering paid work on its output.

## Content Credentials & fingerprint stamping (C2PA — the provenance trail)

Don't just LOG provenance — EMBED it. Every final deliverable (frame, audio, script page)
ships with a signed manifest as durable provenance EVIDENCE when selling the catalog globally:
- **Sign C2PA Content Credentials** into each export (c2patool / Adobe CAI) — author = Ani
  Maghakyan, the creative-contribution note, and the AI model/prompt provenance, cryptographically
  bound to the file. (This is the substance; "blockchain" is marketing — C2PA signing + an
  invisible watermark is what actually holds up.)
- **Ինչ C2PA-ն ՉԻ անում (legal reality):** C2PA-ն հաստատում է ծագման ՄԵՏԱԴԱՏԱՆ
  ամբողջականությունը — ով/ինչ գործիքով/երբ է ստորագրել։ Այն իրավական սեփականություն ՉԻ
  սահմանում և ownership-ը «անվիճելի» ՉԻ դարձնում ([C2PA Explainer](https://c2pa.org/specifications/specifications/2.2/explainer/Explainer.html))։
  Սեփականությունը որոշում են chain of title-ը, պայմանագրերը և հեղինակային իրավունքի օրենքը
  (վերևի բաժինները) — C2PA-ն դրանց ապացուցողական հենարանն է, ոչ փոխարինողը։
- **Invisible watermark + digital fingerprint** (IMATAG-class) for leak-tracing and proof of
  origin independent of metadata stripping.
- Doubles as ONE route to the platform **AI-label** (TikTok reads C2PA for auto-labeling;
  manual disclosure toggle is equally valid — `playbook.md`) — one stamp can serve disclosure
  AND provenance.
- Ties to the rights ledger: every stamped asset gets its Asset ID row (`world-benchmark.md`).

## Likeness & voice (consent before capture)

- **Digital-replica consent in every actor deal memo** (CA AB 2602 / AB 1836-style):
  explicit, specific opt-in for any AI use of a performer's face or voice.
- **Identity-lock gate:** no licensed/owned face enters the AI pipeline without a consent ID
  here first — that ID is the key `ai-pipeline.md`'s identity-lock checks before any generation.
- **AI dubbing of a voice requires separate opt-in** — the diaspora arbitrage play
  (`producer-craft.md` · `localization.md`) depends on these consents existing up front.

## Music

- **Platform "social" licenses do NOT cover sponsored/paid content** — every ՓԼԵՅՍՄԵՆԹ
  post needs a real **sync license**. Generated cues (fal-ai) must have logged prompts.
- **Cue sheet per episode** (cue / source / license / timecode) — owned with
  `sound-craft.md`.

## Clearance & E&O

- **Script clearance** for defamation / right-of-publicity — critical for the
  oligarch-corruption material («Ճերմակ շապիկ»-type); also title clearance.
- **Errors & Omissions cover** before any US distribution ($$$ tier line in
  `producer-craft.md`).

## Sponsor contract clauses (productize safely)

- Category exclusivity · **edit/approval rights limited to the sponsor's OWN scene only,
  never the cut** · make-goods if reach misses · **FTC / ad-disclosure** per territory
  (US-facing posts must disclose).

## Grants & co-production (non-dilutive money)

- Armenian National Cinema Center · **Eurimages** (Armenia is a member) · co-production
  treaties · Creative Armenia / AGBU / Gulbenkian diaspora funding.
- Keep an **application calendar** — most are deadline-gated; missing the window costs a year.
