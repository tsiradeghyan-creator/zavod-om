# Fashion-Editorial Taste — taste canon (photo + AI image/video)

- id: fashion_editorial_taste (card only — not yet in `library/style_radar.json`)
- project: **@dzecir** (Instagram, AI fashion/cinematic visuals + prompts) — see wiki `dzecir.md`. Not Maghakian.
- formed: 2026-10-14 from ~1050 recent posts across 19 Instagram accounts the user curated (list at bottom)
- essence: magazine-grade fashion imagery — **one strong idea per frame, real light, real skin, a single colour accent** — whether shot on camera or generated. The board's top tier is human editorial; the AI accounts are useful only where they imitate that tier.

## Tiers in the reference set (what to learn from whom)
1. **Editorial masters — the taste ceiling.** andreasortnerstudio, elionogueiraofficial, fasheditorials, imgmodels, alana.lucio, noblessekorea.
   Vogue / Bazaar / WSJ / Purple / i-D covers, backstage, campaign tear-sheets.
2. **Art-direction concept — the idea ceiling.** timtadder, black.ai.butterfly, tati_creator.
   One sculptural object or silhouette carries the frame; surreal but disciplined.
3. **Character / street styling — the wardrobe ceiling.** nastya_cheprazova, creepyyeha, maksim_serikow.
   A consistent persona and a signature wardrobe repeated until it becomes a brand.
4. **AI prompt-sellers — technique source, NOT taste source.** its.mrpickard, afzanhilmi, nxxt.ai, aistudio.jessica, tookacreative, olivar.dsgn, sergey_glov.
   Steal the camera physics (long-exposure light trails, 1/8000 freeze-flash, motion-blur codes, slash-presets). Reject their poster packaging.

## Signature markers (the taste, concretely)
- **Light:** motivated and physical — low sun / golden hour (Ortner, Alana's Vogue safari), hard on-camera night flash (Alana, creepyyeha), single hard side light on black (Tadder), soft north-window light (Ortner B/W). Never even, never "studio-lit everything".
- **Colour:** restrained base + **one saturated accent**. Red is the recurring hero accent across ~every tier (Tadder red, tati red, fasheditorials red gowns, Nastya red gloves/tights, Alana red boots). Otherwise: camel, cream, black, bone, dusty sky blue. Full-saturation neon only as a deliberate night register.
- **Black & white** is a first-class register, not a filter — high-contrast portraits, grainy reportage (Ortner, maksim_serikow, Alana).
- **Skin & surface:** visible pores, freckles, fly-away hair, film grain, slight halation. Fabric with real weight (felt, leather, fur, tulle). The board rejects plastic skin and HDR sheen.
- **Composition:** one subject, lots of air; low angle / worm's-eye or tight crop; figure small in vast landscape (Ortner snow, Elio forest); centred iconic symmetry for sculptural pieces (Tadder, black.ai.butterfly).
- **Silhouette as idea:** oversized sculptural volumes — cocoon hoods, disc hats, ruffle bursts, wrap capes (Tadder, tati, Nastya). The garment shape *is* the concept.
- **Narrative props / set:** Elio Nogueira's theatrical worlds (dinosaurs, sheet-music, paper storms, painted rooms) — surreal set-building with couture, still photographed straight.
- **Authenticity cues:** polaroids, contact sheets, backstage, casting cards, phone-in-mirror (Alana, IMG). Mixing "the shoot" with "behind the shoot" reads as real.
- **Type on image (when used):** masthead serif (VOGUE/BAZAAR Didone) small and confident, or nothing. Big white "PROMPT" grotesk stamped over every post = the cheap tell.

## AI / prompt keywords that produce it
`editorial fashion photograph, shot on medium format film, Kodak Portra 400 grain, natural skin texture with visible pores, single motivated light source, low golden-hour sun, deep shadows, muted palette with one saturated red accent, sculptural oversized silhouette, negative space, low worm's-eye angle, Vogue cover composition, slight halation, no retouching sheen`
Night register: `direct on-camera flash, hard shadow behind subject, city light trails from long exposure, subject sharp, background streaked`.
Freeze register: `1/8000s freeze, single hard side flash, suspended droplets/petals against dark background`.
Surreal register: `minimal surreal set, one impossible object, flat painterly backdrop colour, centred symmetric subject, matte textures` (black.ai.butterfly / Tadder).

## When to use
Any fashion / beauty / portrait visual that should read as premium editorial: campaign stills, key art, portraits, carousel covers, Reels opening frames. Brand-neutral — not tied to any client.

## Application rules
- Key art = **tier-1 lighting + tier-2 single idea**: one subject, one object, one saturated accent, real light. Not a collage.
- Portraits: Ortner-style B/W or low-sun colour, real skin, editorial crop — never the glossy "AI influencer" look.
- Type on image: small masthead-style serif, like a magazine cover line — or none. Add text in post, never bake it into the generation.
- For recurring formats use the Nastya / creepyyeha lesson: a fixed persona + fixed wardrobe/location rhythm makes a feed recognisable.

## Anti-pattern (reject on sight)
- Prompt-seller packaging: huge white "PROMPT"/"tutorial" headline over the image, "comment X for the prompt" carousels, before/after split faces (its.mrpickard, afzanhilmi, nxxt.ai, olivar.dsgn, sergey_glov).
- Over-saturated 3D-cartoon / neon-explosion thumbnails (olivar.dsgn) and mascot/meme celebrity renders.
- Product floating on a grey card with gloved hands (sergey_glov) — fine as a technical reference, dead as taste.
- Flat beauty-filtered skin, symmetrical stock smiles, everything in focus, every colour at 100%.
- Collage grids of 9 variations instead of one decisive frame.
