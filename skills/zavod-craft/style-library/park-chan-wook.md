# Park Chan-wook montage — montage

- id: park_chan_wook_graphic_match
- essence: transitions that make two different times/places feel like one continuous space — motivated and geometric, never trend-bait whip.
- signature markers: **graphic match cut** (cut on a shared shape/composition/color so the eye carries across), **spatial match** (a character seems to step into / observe another scene's space — Decision to Leave), match-on-action, a camera move that travels through an object/window into the next space, symmetry, circular/vertical reframing. Editor: Kim Sang-bum.
- when to use: the turn — a memory collapsing into the present, two threads converging, a withheld-truth reveal. Use sparingly, on the beat.
- Maghakian application: her intercut parallel threads → converge structure; bridge past/present on a matching object (the anchor, the frozen laundry line) so the subtext-object carries the cut. Pairs with Peleshyan distance-montage for rhythm.
- anti-pattern: generic whip/zoom transition with no compositional rhyme = the opposite of PCW; a match cut with nothing actually matching.
- reference: Park Chan-wook — Oldboy, The Handmaiden, Decision to Leave.
