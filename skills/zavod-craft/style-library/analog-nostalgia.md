# Analog Nostalgia (film-stock grade) — color_luts

- id: analog_nostalgia
- essence: 2026's #1 grade — emulate real film stock instead of a generic "cinematic" LUT.
- signature markers: visible fine grain + slight **halation** on highlights, gradual highlight roll-off (no hard clip), shadows that retain color & detail (not crushed to pure black), rich reds/yellows (Kodachrome) or soft greens & creamy skin (Portra/Fuji), slight desaturation in extreme highlights.
- AI / CSS keywords / grade: "Kodachrome 64 emulation, fine film grain, halation, gentle highlight rolloff, retained shadow color" · DaVinci: film-print emulation node LAST, grain on top.
- when to use: any piece that should feel remembered, intimate, or premium; pairs with Maghakian's memory/«ապրել vs շնչել» theme.
- Maghakian application: the house warm-gold drama look graded as Portra-warm for memory/flashback beats; keep skin protection (no purple #6450D2 spill) and grain discipline from `color-craft.md`.
- anti-pattern: heavy teal&orange slammed on by default; grain so coarse it reads as digital noise; halation on everything (use on practicals/highlights only).
- reference: Kodak Portra / Kodachrome 64; Wong Kar-wai warmth.
