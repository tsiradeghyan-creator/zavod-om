# Roger Deakins — cinematographers

- id: roger_deakins
- essence: restraint + motivated naturalism — every light has a real-world source; the frame is composed, the move is subtle.
- signature markers: practical/natural light, controlled single-source key, clean strong compositions (often centered or rigorous thirds), minimal but precise camera movement, innovative yet restrained palette (the Blade Runner 2049 monochrome-amber, the 1917 dusk).
- AI / CSS keywords: "motivated single-source lighting, naturalistic, composed wide, subtle dolly, restrained palette, deep negative space".
- when to use: A1/A6 drama & thriller masters, establishing emotional space, the quiet hero frame.
- Maghakian application: the Ani face-to-camera workhorse (big soft key window-side, negative fill) is Deakins-logic; one motivated practical deep in a #0D0D0D room = the house drama look. Build scene_plan.shot_language (lighting_key=low_key, motivated) from this.
- anti-pattern: unmotivated "pretty" lighting, busy camera moves, over-saturated grade — all anti-Deakins.
- reference: Roger Deakins — Blade Runner 2049, 1917, Sicario.
