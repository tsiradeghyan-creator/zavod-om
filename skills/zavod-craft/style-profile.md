## HISTORICAL WORKING NOTES — NOT UNIVERSAL RULES

Use the updated profile above for cross-work claims. The details below can help with matching projects,
but each must be checked against the relevant work and character.
1. **Emotion lives in behaviour, not statement.** Characters never announce feelings; conflict runs
   under the words (Eva jokes to hide terminal illness; Armen wounds to mask despair). Restraint = power.
2. **The cold protagonist who secretly longs to surrender.** Her signature character keeps everyone at
   arm's length, manipulates love, fears attachment — yet aches for the one who "won't let her play
   games." (Anais, Eva, the «Տակնուվրա» narrator.) Title «Տակնուվրա» = upside-down = that secret wish.
3. **The throughline theme: «ապրել vs շնչել»** — to live vs merely breathe — + memory as both wound and
   mercy, dignity after loss, love as the only freedom.
4. **Spoken Yerevan truth + `,,,`.** Real dialect (`ա, սենց, արա, ախպերս, վսյո`), Russian/English code-
   switching by milieu, and the `,,,` trailing beat as her native punctuation. (Literary registers B/C
   switch to clean Eastern Armenian.)
5. **Her recurring symbols & aphorisms** (from «Տակնուվրա»): birds/flight, the anchor («խարիսխը արմատ չէ,
   հույս է»), the frozen laundry line, the saved last cigarette, the flower-girl who lives "here & now,"
   fish/aquarium = loneliness; «Չկա-ն սարսափելի բառ է», «Նայելն ու տեսնելը տարբեր են», «Գնալը հեշտ է,
   ճանապարհողի համար է դժվար». Reuse these — they ARE her, don't invent substitutes.
6. **Structure that hurts:** intercut parallel threads → converge; cliffhanger / gut-punch episode ends
   (someone collapses, a door opens); lyrical first-person voiceover at the turns.
7. **Her exact Armenian screenplay format** (see below) — never Western INT./EXT.

## Screenplay FORMAT (her exact layout — reproduce verbatim)

```
N. ՏԵՍԱՐԱՆ                         ← scene number + the word ՏԵՍԱՐԱՆ
ՎԱՅՐ: ԵՆԹԱՎԱՅՐ: ՑԵՐԵԿ/ԵՐԵԿՈ        ← slugline: LOCATION: SUB-LOC: DAY/EVENING
ԱՆՈՒՆ, ԱՆՈՒՆ                        ← roster of characters in the scene
[Action line — present tense, plain, observational; emotion shown via behaviour.]
ԱՆՈՒՆ /պարենթեզ/                    ← name in CAPS; optional parenthetical in /slashes/
Replika...                          ← dialogue
```

- **Parentheticals in `/.../`** — direction in CAPS (`/ՀԵՌԱՆԱԼՈՎ/`), emotion lowercase (`/հուզված/`,
  `/կոպիտ/`, `/ընդհատելով/`, `/հեգնանքով/`, `/շշուկով/`).
- **Transitions / time cues** on own line: `ՊԵՐԵԽՈԴ`/`ԱՆՑՈՒՄ` (cut), `ՖԼԵՇԲԵՔ`…`ՖԼԵՇԲԵՔԻ ԱՎԱՐՏ`,
  `ՆՈՐ ՖԼԵՇԲԵՔՈՎ ՀԻՇՈՒՄ Է`, `ՕՐ Է ՓՈԽՎՈՒՄ` / `ԴԱՌՆՈՒՄ Է ԵՐԵԿՈ`.
- **Production notes inline in slashes:** `/աբրատնի.../` (reverse-angle), `/ձայն դռան ետևից/`,
  `/արտակադրային ձայն/` (voiceover), `/ըստ լոկացիայի/`, `/ցանկալի կադրը վերևից/`.
- **Continuation marker:** `(ДАЛЬШЕ)` / `ԱՆՈՒՆ (ПРОД.)` (she uses the Russian CONT'D).
- **Sub-scenes** numbered `10.1`, `16.1`. Sponsor scenes flagged `ՓԼԵՅՍՄԵՆԹ` + brand name.

## Dialogue craft

- **Real Yerevan speech** (A-registers): `ա` (=է), `սենց/տենց/ընենց`, `չէ՞`, `հը՞`, `արա`, `ախպերս`,
  `տղերք`, `կարաս`, `բան չկա`, `վսյո`, `պռոստը`. Diaspora layers add English+Russian
  (`բռատ, ինշյուրընս, ռենտ, ուբեր, обратный, всё`).
- **`,,,` (three commas)** = her trailing-off / hesitation beat. Pervasive — it IS her punctuation.
  (Children's & prose registers use full literary punctuation instead.)
- **Subtext over statement** — characters evade, joke, threaten, deflect; the real feeling sits under
  the words (Eva hides terminal illness behind jokes; Armen masks despair with cruelty). Never have a
  character announce their subtext.
- Short volleys, interruptions, overlaps (`/X-ի հետ միաժամանակ/`). **Emotion lives in action lines**
  («զսպում է հուզմունքը», «կախում է հայացքը», «ձգված ժպիտը մի վայրկյանում կորում է»), not in adverbs.
- **Running gags / verbal tics** per character (Markosyan's «եղբայր»; Levon's deadpan one-liners).

## Structure & storytelling DNA

- **Daily-series episode** = ~20–40 short one-beat scenes, fast location cuts.
- **Multiple parallel storylines intercut** per episode, converging later (e.g. in «քեզանից հետո»:
  Eva⟂Rob + Arsen⟂Sofi + Edgar⟂Anna run simultaneously).
- **Flashbacks** for backstory (war, the inciting crime), bracketed by her cues.
- **Cliffhanger / gut-punch episode endings** — end on the turn (Eva faints; police at the door),
  not the resolution. Many episodes literally end with someone collapsing or a door opening.
- **Voiceover montages** at episode turns (silent scene + `/արտակադրային ձայն/`) carrying the theme.
- **Anthology trick** («84 ամառ»): same actor recurs as different characters; note costume continuity.
- **End-revealed framing** (her most cinematic move, «Ճերմակ շապիկ»): recurring "unclear-location"
  voiceover scenes whose true context is revealed only in the finale, recontextualizing the whole story.
- **Blogger / social-media chorus** («Ճերմակ շապիկ», «selavi»): recurring `ՏԵՍԱՆՅՈՒԹ`/influencer cutaways
  act as a public-conscience Greek chorus + plot engine. Time-cards: «ՄԵՐ ՕՐԵՐ», «ՕՐԵՐ ԱՆՑ», «… ՏԱՐԻ ԱՆՑ».

## Drafting shorthand & reels
- Her working drafts mix **full-dialogue scenes** with **scene-skeleton shorthand** (slugline +
  roster + a prose paragraph summarizing the beat). Fine to draft fast in skeleton, then expand.
- She deliberately writes scenes so they can be **cut into reels/clips** (in-script note:
  «ավելի ձիգ տեքստ գրենք որ ռիլ դառնա»). Keep beats tight and clip-able — pairs with the zavod's
  video station.

## Prose register (C) — for «Տակնուվրա»-type narration
First person; poetic restraint; **a recurring physical symbol** carries the theme (frozen laundry
line = home/dignity); repetition for grief («Չկա-ն սարսափելի բառ է»); sensory detail (cold, hunger);
aphoristic turns; love framed as freedom. Same moral universe as the screenplays.

## Recurring themes (her world)
Family duty & sacrifice · dignity after loss · war trauma (Artsakh/44-day war, blockade, wheelchair
vets) · "ապրել vs շնչել" (to live vs merely breathe) · love as freedom / living fully under a death

## Writing-for-her checklist
- [ ] Correct register (A1–A4 colloquial / B literary-kids / C prose).
- [ ] Her exact screenplay format: `N. ՏԵՍԱՐԱՆ` → slugline `: ՑԵՐԵԿ/ԵՐԵԿՈ` → roster → action → `ԱՆՈՒՆ /.../`.
- [ ] Dialogue sounds spoken (dialect + `,,,`); conflict via subtext; emotion in action lines.
- [ ] Transitions/flashbacks marked with HER cues. Proper Armenian Unicode throughout.
- [ ] Daily-series episode → intercut threads + a cliffhanger ending.

## Anti-patterns (NOT her voice)
- Sterile/literary dialogue in an A-register drama (must sound like the street).
- Western format (INT./EXT., CUT TO:) — she uses `ՏԵՍԱՐԱՆ` / `ՑԵՐԵԿ` / `ՊԵՐԵԽՈԴ`.
- Characters explaining their feelings; resolving the episode instead of cliff-hanging.
- "Correcting" the `,,,` or the Russian/English slang — that flattens her voice.
- Generic AI melodrama; she earns emotion through restraint + specific behaviour.

## What she NEVER writes (negative-space rules)
- Stated morals / a character announcing the theme.
- Weather-opening scenes ("it was a cold morning…") — she opens on behaviour.
- Two consecutive resolution scenes — there is always a new turn between reliefs.
- Adverb-laden parentheticals (`/very sadly/`) — direction is CAPS, emotion is one
  lowercase word, the rest lives in the action line.

## Per-character idiolect sheet (fill one per major character)
Voice is individual, not just register. For each lead, lock:
- **Verbal tic / signature word** (Markosyan's «եղբայր»; Levon's deadpan).
- **Taboo words** — what this character would never say.
- **Sentence length** — clipped vs. spilling; interrupts or finishes others.
- **Code-switch ratio** — how much Russian/English by milieu (A2 diaspora high, A1 low).
- **`,,,` density** — who trails off (the evasive, the wounded) vs who lands clean lines.

## Scene-rhythm metrics (measurable, so a draft is checkable not just "vibey")
- **Lines per volley:** ~1–2 in A-registers; short, overlapping, interrupted.
- **Scenes per daily-serial episode:** ~20–40 one-beat scenes, fast location cuts.
- **`,,,` and code-switch frequency** are per-register targets, not decoration — A2/A3
  high, B/C near zero. A draft that misses the density misses the voice.

## Machine-draft QA (the self-improving station's gate)
- **Dialogue-pass:** every draft gets one pass that cuts the FIRST and LAST line of each
  exchange — she enters late, leaves early; the cut usually improves it.
- **Aphorism usage ledger:** track each signature aphorism's last use; **cooldown before
  reuse** (reusing «Չկա-ն սարսափելի բառ է» every third post = self-parody). Reuse honestly,
  not reflexively.
- **Golden-sample Turing QA:** periodically show Ani a machine excerpt beside a real one.
  If she can't tell which is hers, the station passes; if she can, capture WHY here.
- **Confidence flag:** voice capture is strongest for A1–A6; treat **register B and the
  standalones as PROVISIONAL** (novel pp.49–160 + several episodes still unread).
