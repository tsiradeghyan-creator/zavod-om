## Logline
A man travels to the USA to finally meet the woman he loves — and discovers she has been
presenting herself under a **false identity** the whole time (Armenian synopsis: she is
already married; English synopsis: she is about to marry another man). Core conflict:
**love → deception → false identity → truth revealed in America.**

## Themes / analytical layers
1. **The illusion of distant love** — he believes enough to cross an ocean for it.
2. **False-identity reveal** — the woman he loved never really existed; the psychological hit.
3. **America as the space of truth** — not just a location; where dream collides with reality.
4. **The «Խաղալիք» metaphor** — he was played with, used as a *toy*. (Strong editorial angle;
   not a literal line from the official synopsis.)
5. **Love vs. dignity** — what a person does when the foundation of their love turns out a lie.
