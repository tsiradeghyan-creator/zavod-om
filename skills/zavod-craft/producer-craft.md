# Producer Craft — Պրոդյուսերական վարպետություն (FILM MODE)

World-class producing: development → financing → pre-production → set → distribution,
tuned to the agency's reality (daily-series economics, ՓԼԵՅՍՄԵՆԹ financing, diaspora
markets, AI prototyping at near-zero cost).

## Development & rights

- **Chain of title first:** every script needs a signed transfer/option (the corpus
  already holds her contract templates — «Հեղինակային իրավունքների փոխանցման
  պայմանագիր»). No deck goes out before rights are clean.
- **Pitch deck anatomy (8 pages, never more):** logline → world & tone (one image per
  register) → 3 characters (want/wound/secret) → season arc in 6 turns → comps
  ("X meets Y", one local + one global) → audience & platform → budget range → the team
  (aggregate portfolio numbers from `filmography.md` — never one show).
- **Pitch-Builder (auto-compile the deck):** give a logline + 3 character names → the deck
  auto-fills from the bench: moodboards from `ai-pipeline.md` (no-faces, on-palette), audience
  + comps from the Audience-Risk Card (`world-benchmark.md`), budget from the tiers below,
  rights/licensing notes from `business-affairs.md`, team page from `filmography.md` aggregate.
  Export a buyer-ready **PDF/X** (Kinodaran, TV channels, vertical portals). Per the automation
  rule, the agent compiles; the human does the **Maghakian veto** — rights status, voice fit,
  and the final numbers are human-verified, never auto-approved.
- **Proof-of-concept = AI sizzle:** a 40–60s teaser rendered via `cinematography.md`
  prompt tables + fal-ai costs ~nothing and beats 10 pages of prose in any pitch room.
  (Idea gates G1–G4 live in `idea-lab.md`.)
- **Sizzle vs proof-of-tone — bring BOTH for drama buyers:** the AI sizzle proves the
  *premise*; but drama is bought on *tone*, and AI render hides her real asset — the
  dialogue. Pair the sizzle with ONE fully-produced 60–90s real-actor scene.
- **Buyer map (know who you're pitching):** local channels / USArmenia (TV); vertical
  submission portals ReelShort · DramaBox · **NetShort** (2026's #3 leader) · ShortMax ·
  FlexTV; RU-language platforms for the Moscow diaspora. Match the deck's comps and budget
  tier to the buyer — and note DramaBox's sub-heavy model (70% subscription revenue) vs
  ReelShort's coin+UA model when arguing the deal structure.
- **Option agreement basics** (especially the novel «Տակնուվրա»): option fee 3–5% of
  purchase price, 12–18 month term + paid extension, reversion clause, separated rights
  (publishing / stage / sequel held back). Templates → `business-affairs.md`.

## Format licensing — the catalog goldmine (biggest untapped revenue)

47 projects / ~2,270 episodes is a remake-rights catalog, not just a résumé.
- **FIRST: catalog-rights audit.** Which of the 47 does Ani actually control vs.
  work-for-hire owned by the channels? This single answer determines the whole strategy.
  File it in `business-affairs.md`.
- **Format bible per controllable title:** the engine, episode grammar, casting
  archetypes, ratings history — the document a foreign producer needs to remake it.
- **Paper format vs scripted format sale:** license the *format* (local remake) or sell
  finished episodes with dubbing.
- **Target markets:** Georgia, Kazakhstan, the Balkans, MENA (dubbing). Her daily-serial
  engines travel well; the diaspora registers (A2/A3) pre-localize themselves.

## Budgeting (tiers the agency actually uses)

| Tier | What | Line items that matter |
|---|---|---|
| $ — AI prototype | sizzle, micro-drama pilot | render credits, VO session, music license, post days |
| $$ — smartphone/one-light | Reels, face-to-camera, poster-prop shoots | location, props, one crew day, food |
| $$$ — pro short/episode | channel work | cast, 3–5 crew, location package, sound, color, insurance |

Iron rules: 10% contingency untouched until the last week · post is never the line you
cut · if a scene costs more than its turn is worth dramatically, the producer asks the
writer for a cheaper turn (she can write it — see ՓԼԵՅՍՄԵՆԹ craft below).
**$$$ tier adds an E&O / insurance line:** her oligarch-corruption material needs script
clearance + Errors & Omissions cover against defamation / right-of-publicity before any
US-distributed release (`business-affairs.md`).

## Scheduling (daily-series math)

- **Script breakdown first:** strip every scene into cast/location/props/SFX — her
  format makes this easy (slugline roster = breakdown sheet).
- **Stripboard + Day-out-of-Days:** cast efficiency is the #1 cost lever in daily-serial
  blocks — schedule to minimize the days each actor is held; the DOOD sheet drives the
  deal memos. The 1st AD owns this (`production-team.md`).
- **Shoot by location, not by story order.** Group all scenes per set across episodes.
- **Daily-series pace:** ~30–40 pages/day is the local industry reality her scripts are
  built for (short one-beat scenes = the schedule's friend). Protect the TURN scenes:
  schedule them early in the day, never last.
- **Daily hot-cost report** vs budget; **deal memos** for all cast/crew before they work;
  completion contingency 10% (+ ~2% bond fee if a completion bond is required).
- Call sheet minimum: scenes & pages, cast in/out, location + parking, weather,
  sunset time, nearest hospital, one line of tomorrow.

## ՓԼԵՅՍՄԵՆԹ / sponsor integration (her existing superpower → revenue)

Her scripts already flag «ՓԼԵՅՍՄԵՆԹ» scenes inline — productize it:
- **Rate card with named tiers + price logic:** prop placement < verbal mention <
  plot-woven scene (the «Folk-Marta venue» model from «քեզանից հետո»); price = tier ×
  episode reach.
- Producer's rule: the sponsor scene must still pass the scene test (objective/turn) —
  if it's an ad, it dies in the edit; if it's a scene, it survives forever.
- **Measurement & report:** unique promo codes, UTM links in caption, brand-lift
  mini-surveys → a one-page report deck per sponsor.
- **Brand-safety tiers:** spell out which storylines a sponsor can / can't sit inside.
- US-facing posts need **FTC ad disclosure**; contract clauses → `business-affairs.md`.
- Keep a one-page placement menu with reach numbers (aggregate from `playbook.md`).

## Distribution & markets

- **TV (the day job):** local channels + USArmenia — deliverables per their specs.
- **Vertical micro-drama (the bet) — economics re-verified 2026-07:** seasons run
  **50–100 (up to 120) episodes × 1–2 min** (typically ~100–200 min total); free **5–10 episodes**,
  then ~$0.30–0.50/episode coin unlocks or ~$17–20/week subs (+ ad-unlock tier: watch ads →
  ~6 episodes/24h). A series costs **$100–300K** (do the per-minute division per project, don't
  quote a flat rate), **but marketing runs ≈9× production cost** (60–70% of installs bought on
  Facebook/TikTok ads) — so the binding constraint is NOT per-episode cost, it's UA. Proof:
  ReelShort grossed ~$1.2B (70M+ MAU) and is still LOSS-MAKING on marketing, while DramaBox
  ($323M rev 2024, 50M+ MAU) turned a $10M profit with **70% of revenue from subscriptions** —
  subs beat coins for margin. **The indie play is selling TO a platform (flat fee / rev-share),
  not self-distributing.** Market: in-app revenue $3.8B (2025) → $7.8B forecast 2026 (Deloitte);
  total-industry estimates $11–14B by end-2026; ReelShort + DramaBox ≈70% of the global market.
  **Platform tempo norm:** 1 month development · 1 week shoot · 1 week post; vertical crews
  average 10–15 script pages/day — her 30–40-page daily-serial muscle is a structural advantage.
  The «Վանը»/«Ախրիման» universe is the natural candidate (`serial-structure.md` beat math ·
  `playbook.md` monetization).
- **🇦🇲 Armenian arbitrage play (own an unserved niche):** AI dubbing now cuts localization
  cost up to ~90%. Shoot ONCE in Armenian at Yerevan production costs (far below the $150K
  LA floor), then AI-dub RU/EN for Moscow + LA/Glendale diaspora (`localization.md`), and
  pitch the package as **pre-localized**. Nobody serves Armenian-language verticals — own
  the niche, then use it as proof-of-format for bigger-language format sales (above).
- **Owned channels:** YouTube/Kinodaran windows after TV; clips → Reels (one source,
  native per platform — `playbook.md`).
- **Diaspora targeting:** LA/Glendale + Moscow audiences are HER registers (A2/A3) —
  geo-tag, time posts to their evenings (`playbook.md` window), subtitle EN/RU per
  `localization.md`.
- **Grants & co-production:** Armenian National Cinema Center, Eurimages (Armenia is a
  member), co-production treaties, Creative Armenia / AGBU / Gulbenkian diaspora funding —
  application calendar in `business-affairs.md`.
